# QR Code Generator

**A practical QR code, without unnecessary design decisions.**

QR codes are useful for sharing a link, adding information to packaging, or giving someone quick access to a page. Creating one should be straightforward, without turning it into another design project.

## The Approach

I wanted the QR code to do its job quietly, especially when it's part of something that's already been designed.

- **Start simple.** Enter a link or text and get a standard black-and-white QR code.
- **Customize when it matters.** Add a small logo or change the colors without having to navigate a wall of options.
- **Ready to use.** Download a PNG for everyday sharing or an SVG for layouts and printing.

The focus is on a QR code that's easy to create, easy to scan, and easy to fit into someone else's work.

## Privacy

QR codes and uploaded logos are processed in your browser. The generator doesn't upload your input or logo to a server for processing.
